Building trust into money movement

Finastra · 2022–2025
0-to-1 launch of a B2B payment initiation platform

Challenge

SMB customers needed a way to move money that was fast, but couldn't compromise on control — every transfer needed to be verifiable and hard to get wrong, by design, not by policy.

Approach

Led discovery and owned the product end-to-end: designed a secure domestic wire transfer flow with maker-checker approval built into the core workflow, then extended it with an FX transfer flow across the UX and API layers. Ran the joint initiative with Visa on Banking-as-a-Service capabilities, aligning partner, compliance, and technical stakeholders across geographies.

Outcome

A regulated, trust-sensitive product shipped from scratch — built and validated together with Engineering, Design, Sales, Customer Success, Legal, and Compliance.
